What "reliable" means in my pipelines

          
    
            
  • Schema as a contract
    Explicit types from the legacy record format, never inferred. Drift fails loudly.
    • 
            
  • No silent data loss
    Null-safe predicates; every rejected row goes to quarantine with a reason.
    • 
            
  • Idempotent re-runs
    Dynamic partition overwrite: re-running a day replaces only that day.
    • 
            
  • Parity before cut-over
    Counts, checksums and field-level diffs against legacy output.
    • 
            
  • Config-driven, not copy-paste
    One shared engine and small per-feed rule modules. Repetitive runners are generated, not hand-written.
    • 
            
  • Safe to operate
    Dry-run and debug modes, stage-level row counts and structured logs for on-call.
    •
